# Adaptations from AzerothCore

Each entry is a deliberate difference from AzerothCore behaviour, or a rule AzerothCore does not
define (player behaviour). Tests named in the last column pin the behaviour.

| ID | Rule | Reason | Test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-001 | Events at the same millisecond run in classes: spell hits, periodic ticks, aura expiries, AI updates, player decisions; within a class in schedule order. | AzerothCore order depends on map update internals; we need a fixed, documented order (§8.2). | `queue.test.ts`, `sim.test.ts` |
| A-002 | The boss AI update runs at a fixed interval (default 10 ms, an `assumption` in `core-data.ts`). | AzerothCore's diff varies with server load. | `boss.test.ts` |
| A-003 | Distances are 2D. Range between units = centre distance − both combat reaches, never negative. Area membership = centre distance from the area centre ≤ radius; the unit's combat reach is added only when a player caster hits a creature (Spell.cpp 9504–9521). Areas are centred on the caster or on the explicit target and select units by their relation to the caster (enemy, ally, any; optionally players only, optionally without the caster). | Festergut's room is flat; AzerothCore measures area distances in 3D. | `movement.test.ts`, `area.test.ts`, `auras.test.ts` |
| A-004 | Movement is a straight line at constant speed, no pathing. | Festergut's room has no obstacles. | `movement.test.ts` |
| A-005 | Players react to plan rule triggers after a reaction delay drawn per player; rotation decisions have no delay. | Players are not part of AzerothCore. | `rules.test.ts` |
| A-006 | Instant spells resolve inside the event that cast them. | Equivalent to AzerothCore's immediate spell handling for instant casts without travel time. | `casting.test.ts` |
| A-007 | Random numbers come from named streams per mechanic and per actor purpose. | AzerothCore uses one global generator; we need comparable runs (§8.7). | `rng.test.ts`, `run.test.ts` |
| A-008 | Healing a dead unit has no effect and writes no event. | Death is final within the millisecond (§8.2). | `sim.test.ts` |
| A-009 | Refreshing a stacking aura resets its duration and keeps its periodic tick schedule. | Simplification until a mechanic needs otherwise. | `auras.test.ts` |
| A-010 | Plan rule actions compete only for movement: a rule move pre-empts a running rule move of lower priority; other actions run regardless. | Instant actions such as taunt do not conflict with movement in the game. | `rules.test.ts` |
| A-011 | Stand-ins act once per second, produce nothing while moving, and use fixed ranges (melee and tank 5 yd, ranged and healer 40 yd; all parameters are `assumption` entries in `core-data.ts`). A healer stand-in heals the injured player in range with the lowest health percentage (not lowest absolute health), ties broken by id. | Stand-ins are approximations by definition (§6.3). | `standin.test.ts` |
| A-012 | Removal of an aura by death fires neither its `onExpire` effects nor the `afterRemove` hooks. | Generic rule; until a dedicated hook exists (Plan 2), a boss script can handle the specific aura via `sim.subscribe`. | `auras.test.ts` |
| A-013 | A new move_start ends any previous move without a move_end; death ends a running cast and move without cast_interrupted/move_end (the death event closes them). | Keeps the trace small; consumers pair starts with the next move_start, move_end, cast_*, or death. | `sim.test.ts` |
| A-014 | Policy decisions: a move does not end the decision (an instant may follow); a policy may ask to be woken at a later time; more than 64 decisions by one actor in one millisecond is an error. | Kiting casters and timer-gated policies; wake loops must fail loudly. | `controller.test.ts` |
| A-015 | health_below is evaluated on damage and heal events of the watched unit; the killing blow does not fire it (the unit is dead). | Rules react to the living. | `rules.test.ts` |
| A-016 | Auras stack per caster by default: an instance is keyed by aura and caster (`aura|caster`), so two casters' copies coexist and only the same caster's copy stacks and refreshes. Stack scope `unit` keeps one instance per unit that any caster stacks. Scope `unit_replace` keeps one instance per unit that its own caster stacks, while another caster's application removes it (reason `replaced`, afterRemove runs with the old instance's times) before the new one is applied with freshly fixed periodic amounts (area auras of different casters, SpellAuras.cpp 2131–2151, 2193–2210). | One rule per scope: per-caster debuffs, shared debuffs and AzerothCore's area-aura rule. | `auras.test.ts`, `aura-scripts.test.ts`, `aura-replace.test.ts` |
| A-017 | Damage and healing amounts are rounded half-up to whole numbers before they are applied; spell damage is first truncated after each modifier step (damage done, damage taken, area reduction; A-026). | Health is an integer; one deterministic rounding rule. | `sim.test.ts` |
| A-018 | A spell's cooldown starts when the cast succeeds, not when it begins. | A cast that fails or is interrupted does not consume the cooldown. | `casting.test.ts` |
| A-019 | Threat: the victim is reselected every 1 s (ThreatManager.h line 94), immediately when a taunt starts or ends, and immediately when the victim dies (the death event is the cause); fixate is not modelled; equal threat goes to the lowest id. Damage threat uses the source's `threatMultiplier` only; healing threat is split over all living creatures listing the healed unit (no crowd-control states). One shared 1 s tick is used instead of per-creature timers reset by taunt. | Deterministic port of ThreatManager; fixate is not needed for Festergut. | `threat.test.ts`, `taunt.test.ts` |
| A-020 | Triggered casts ignore cast time, GCD, cooldown, the casting state and range. | Matches the use of `CastSpell(..., true)` in the Festergut script; to be checked against TriggerCastFlags per spell in Plan 2b. | `boss-targeting.test.ts` |
| A-021 | Boss target selection measures distance edge to edge (A-003) and picks uniformly at random from its own stream; SelectTarget's position argument is not modelled. | Same distance rule as everywhere else. | `boss-targeting.test.ts` |
| A-022 | Boss melee: miss, dodge and parry from the victim's configured avoidance; no crits, crushing blows, glancing blows or block; no leeway range bonus; the swing timer is ready at engage. The table roll is `urand(0, 10000)` (0..10000, a roll equal to the avoidance total is a hit). Damage order like CalculateMeleeDamage: damage done, then damage taken (each truncated), then armor with `ceil` (CalcArmorReducedDamage); avoidance chances are converted like AzerothCore `int32(chance * 100.0f)` (float32, truncated). | Avoidance comes from stand-in profiles until Plan 3 models player defense. | `melee.test.ts` |
| A-023 | The plan action `taunt` uses generic values (30 yd, 8 s cooldown, 3 s) for every actor. | Until Plan 3 gives each spec its own taunt spell from client data. | `taunt.test.ts` |
| A-024 | Spell resolution (Spell::_cast, research §6): every effect selects its units at launch; per unit (in order of first appearance: effect order, area units by id) and per effect, direct damage is fixed with the modifiers in effect then. Each unit is then hit: `spell_hit`, the spell's auras, then per effect the `onEffectHitTarget` hook before the effect's default handler, then the fixed damage, then `afterHitTarget`. A travelling spell hits all its units when it reaches the explicit target. Aura scripts run after the apply, stack or remove event. Hooks are not called when the holder/target is dead; `previousStacks` is given to aura hooks. Hooks cannot change the damage fixed at launch (there is no SetHitDamage equivalent); a hook that kills the unit stops its remaining handlers. A travelling spell hits all its units when it reaches the explicit target, using that target's travel time for every unit. | AzerothCore's order (HandleLaunchPhase, DoSpellHitOnUnit, HandleEffects HIT_TARGET); first-appearance order stands in for its internal target list order. | `aura-scripts.test.ts`, `spell-pipeline.test.ts` |
| A-025 | A boss's kill set defaults to the primary boss and must name existing units. | Putricide-style scripted units that must not be killed need an explicit set; a typo must fail loudly. | `run.test.ts` |
| A-026 | Area damage avoidance: `aoe_damage_taken_pct` (MOD_AOE_DAMAGE_AVOIDANCE) multiplies damage that reaches the target through an area, directly or as a tick of an aura an area applied; `creature_aoe_damage_taken_pct` (MOD_CREATURE_AOE_DAMAGE_AVOIDANCE) does the same only when the casting unit is a creature (Unit::CalculateAOEDamageReduction, Spell.cpp 8722–8729, SpellAuraEffects.cpp 6398–6405). Spell damage is truncated to a whole number after each step in AzerothCore's order: damage done, damage taken, then the area reduction (see A-017). | Single-target damage and melee are not area damage; per-step truncation follows AzerothCore and leaves existing traces unchanged because they apply no spell modifiers. | `aoe-modifiers.test.ts` |
| A-027 | Max targets: when more units qualify for an area than its limit (MaxAffectedTargets, or the cast's `maxTargets` like SPELLVALUE_MAX_TARGETS), a random subset is drawn from a named stream (default `unit:<caster>.<spell>.targets`, or the cast's `targetStream`); no number is drawn otherwise. Effects of one spell with identical targeting share one unit list (Spell.cpp 1002–1011, 1414–1419). An area in an aura tick or on-expire effect uses its own `AreaEffect.maxTargets` and the stream `unit:<caster>.<aura>.targets`; cast overrides do not apply there. An invalid `targetStream` is rejected when the cast starts. | Named streams keep selections comparable between runs (A-007). | `max-targets.test.ts` |
| A-028 | A player's area damage is scaled by 10 / units when the spell hits more than 10 units (Spell.cpp 8730–8735); the scaled amount is truncated to a whole number after the damage-done, damage-taken and area-reduction steps, like each of them. Spells never crit: creature spells cannot (Unit.cpp 8896–8897); player-cast spells could (Gastric Explosion), which is not modelled until Plan 3 adds crit streams. `ignoreCasterDamageMods` (SPELL_ATTR6_IGNORE_CASTER_DAMAGE_MODIFIERS) drops the caster's damage-done factors (SpellInfo.cpp 1655–1674). | Crit chances belong to the class models. | `player-aoe.test.ts` |
| A-029 | Periodic damage and healing amounts are rolled once and fixed whenever the aura is applied, stacked or refreshed (AuraEffect::CalculateAmount, SpellAuraEffects.cpp 557–580): for damage, the rolled amount is multiplied by the caster's damage-done factor and truncated (A-017), then multiplied by the stacks; healing is the roll × stacks. At each tick the damage-taken factor and then, for an aura an area applied, the area reduction are applied to the fixed amount, each truncated (6398–6405). `ignoreCasterDamageMods` on an aura skips the done step. Recalculating on a refresh without a stack change is an assumption. | AzerothCore computes aura amounts at application and the victim's factors at each tick; per-step truncation as in A-017 and A-026. | `periodic-amounts.test.ts` |
